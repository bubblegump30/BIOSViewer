# Code of Conduct

BIOS Viewer is maintained by Purple Dragon Foundation Ltd. Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces is expected to help keep the community welcoming and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.
- Give constructive feedback about the work and accept corrections thoughtfully.
- Respect other people's privacy and consent.
- Keep discussions relevant and make room for different perspectives.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing someone's private information without consent are not permitted. These expectations also apply when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on abusive content. For non-sensitive moderation questions, open an issue and mention [@bubblegump30](https://github.com/bubblegump30). Do not post private details about an incident in a public issue. This repository does not publish a dedicated confidential conduct-reporting inbox.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, give a warning, limit participation, or block an account depending on severity and repeated behavior. Moderation decisions should be proportionate and protect affected participants. A participant may request review of a decision without repeating harmful or private content publicly.

Reports of software vulnerabilities belong in the private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
